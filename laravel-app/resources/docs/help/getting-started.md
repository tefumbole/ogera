# Getting Started

Welcome to **Ogera**. This guide explains the day-to-day screens your team uses most. Open **Help** any time from the left sidebar.

## Sign in

1. Open [ogeragency.com](https://ogeragency.com) and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your **username** (or email) and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

![Login screen](/public/help/screenshots/01-login.png)

4. If your role requires it, enter the **6-digit OTP** sent to your WhatsApp, then **Verify OTP**.

![OTP screen](/public/help/screenshots/01b-otp.png)

5. You land on the **Dashboard**.

> Keep your password private. If you forget it, ask an administrator to reset it under **People → User List**.

![Dashboard](/public/help/screenshots/02-dashboard.png)

## The sidebar

The left menu is organised by module. Menus you see depend on your **role permissions**.

| Menu | What it is for |
|------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | Today’s summary and shortcuts |
| **Help** | This user guide |
| **Product** | Catalogue, barcodes, stock count |
| **Sale** | POS and sales invoices |
| **Rental Module** | Bookings, contracts, reminders |
| **People** | Customers, suppliers, users |
| **Announcements** | WhatsApp messages and schedules |
| **Settings** | Company, timezone, messaging, roles |

## Open Help later

Click **Help** in the sidebar (question-mark icon, just under Dashboard). Pick a chapter, or use **Print** on any page if you want a paper copy.

> Core chapters in this first release: Getting Started, Products, Sales & POS, Rental Module, People, Announcements, Settings. More menus will be added later.

## Tips for every screen

- Use the **search** boxes — most lists filter as you type.
- The **Action** button on a row opens print, edit, WhatsApp, and more.
- Red / green badges on menus mean items need attention (e.g. booking requests).
