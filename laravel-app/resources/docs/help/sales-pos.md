# Sales & POS

Use **Sale** for counter sales and the full sales list. POS is the fastest path when a customer is in front of you.

![POS](/public/help/screenshots/07-pos.png)

## Open POS

1. Sidebar → **Sale** → **POS**.
2. Select **Customer** (and warehouse if asked).
3. Add products:
   - Type a **name** or **barcode** in the product search box, or
   - Scan a barcode — the product is added when the scanner presses Enter.
4. Change quantities on each line if needed.
5. Choose payment method and amount.
6. Complete the sale. You can print the invoice from the confirmation or from the sale list later.

> Digital and service products appear in search too — not only warehouse stock.

## Sale list

![Sale list](/public/help/screenshots/08-sales-list.png)

1. Sidebar → **Sale** → **Sale List**.
2. Find a sale by reference, customer, or date.
3. **Action** menu typically offers:
   - View / print invoice
   - Add payment
   - Send by WhatsApp / email (when messaging is configured)
   - Return or edit (if your role allows)

## Add sale (form)

Sidebar → **Sale** → **Add Sale** is the longer form (useful when you need more detail than POS). The product search works the same way: name, code, or scanner.

## Print and signatures

On the invoice view, use the **print** icon. Admin and client signature areas appear on the printed layout when signatures are on file for the user / client.

## Gift cards & coupons

If your role includes them, they appear under **Sale → Gift Card List** and **Coupon List**. Apply a gift card or coupon on the POS / sale payment step.
